NYALEE - easy phone helper for elders (v4.0). English only, no PIN. Android Studio project.

BUILD
1. Android Studio > Open > select this "nyalee" folder. Let Gradle sync (needs internet once).
2. Build > Build Bundle(s)/APK(s) > Build APK(s). APK: app/build/outputs/apk/debug/.
3. Install. Open Nyalee and LONG-PRESS the word "Nyalee" (caregiver menu, no PIN):
   - SOS number(s)                      -> used by SOS and "I'm OK"
   - up to 4 family people              -> big coloured call tiles on the home screen
   - Emergency info                     -> name, blood group, conditions, medicines, home (shown by the "My Info" tile)
   - Speak button names ON/OFF, Text size, set as Home screen, Play Store

WHAT IT DOES
- Home: SOS, Talk (voice), family tiles, I'm OK, My Info, Phone, Messages, WhatsApp, YouTube, Photos, Camera,
  Contacts, M-Pesa, Torch, Medicine reminder, Emergency Numbers, More Tools, Settings, Voice Help.
- Tap the name "Nyalee" to hear the time, date and battery. Say "what time is it" or "battery level" to Talk.
- Taps are debounced and vibrate lightly; buttons speak their name (can be switched off).

OFFLINE VOICE
- Voice Help guides are spoken by the phone's own text-to-speech (no internet). Install the English voice:
  Settings > Text-to-speech.
- Talk asks for OFFLINE speech recognition. Download the English pack once:
  Settings > Google > Voice > Offline speech recognition (English).
